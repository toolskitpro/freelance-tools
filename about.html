👋 About ToolsKitPro

Free, accurate tools built for freelancers who want to charge what they're worth.

Our Mission

ToolsKitPro was created to solve a simple problem: freelancers don't know what to charge. Whether you're a designer in London, a developer in Toronto, or a writer in New York — figuring out your hourly rate shouldn't require a finance degree.

We build free, no-signup calculators that help freelancers in the US, UK, and Canada set fair rates, plan taxes, generate invoices, and manage their finances — all without sharing personal data.

What We Offer

  • Hourly Rate Calculator — Factor in taxes, expenses, and vacation time
  • Invoice Generator — Create professional PDF invoices in seconds
  • Tax Estimator — Estimate self-employment tax for US, UK & Canada
  • Project Quote Calculator — Price projects confidently
  • Savings Planner — Plan for irregular freelance income

Who We Serve

Our tools are designed for web developers, designers, writers, marketers, consultants, virtual assistants, and any self-employed professional in the US, UK, or Canada.

Our Story

ToolsKitPro started in 2026 when we noticed that most freelance rate calculators were either too simple (ignoring taxes and expenses) or too complex (requiring account creation and data sharing). We wanted something in the middle: powerful enough to be accurate, simple enough to use in 60 seconds.

Today, we're building the most trusted suite of freelance financial tools on the web — one calculator at a time.
