💡 Pro Tips for Freelancers

  • Charge 20% more than the calculated rate to account for non-billable time (proposals, admin).
  • Raise rates annually — inflation and experience growth matter.
  • Package deals often earn more than hourly (e.g., $2,000 for a project vs $50/hr).
  • Track your time — use tools like Toggl or Clockify (free).
  • Save 25-30% of every payment for taxes if self-employed.
